# Skill — Video Trim and Polish

## Goal

Turn Andrew's raw recording into a watchable cut without changing the message.

## Input

- raw video;
- script or transcript if available;
- target platform;
- desired duration;
- notes from Andrew.

## Rules

Cut reading pauses, long silences, false starts, repeated takes, and dead air.

Keep natural micro-pauses, useful facial reactions, and human moments if they improve trust.

Do not over-edit into robotic cadence.

## Output

Return:

- trim plan;
- list of cuts;
- sections needing B-roll/overlay;
- captions notes;
- final export checklist.
