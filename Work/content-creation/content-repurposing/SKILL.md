# Skill — Content Repurposing

## Goal

Turn one idea into platform-specific assets without changing the thesis.

## Inputs

- original idea;
- pillar;
- bucket;
- primary asset;
- proof/example;
- target platforms.

## Output

Return:

- LinkedIn post;
- X post;
- carousel outline;
- short video script;
- optional lead magnet angle;
- what should not be repurposed.
