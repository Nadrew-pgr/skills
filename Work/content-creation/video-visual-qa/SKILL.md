# Skill — Video Visual QA

## Goal

Review a video or storyboard before publication/export.

## Checks

- First 2 seconds stop attention.
- Visual/text/audio hook align.
- Main idea is clear on mute.
- Captions readable on mobile.
- No cheap generic AI neon look.
- Motion clarifies the concept.
- Brand colors/hierarchy consistent.
- Pacing is not slow.
- Pacing is not over-edited.
- CTA is clear if present.
- No unsupported claims.

## Output

Return:

- pass/fail;
- top 3 fixes only;
- publication risk;
- whether human review is needed.
