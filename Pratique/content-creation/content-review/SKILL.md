# Skill — Content Review

## Goal

Review a content asset before publication.

## Checks

- Fits one pillar.
- Fits one bucket.
- One idea only.
- Hook is clear.
- Example/proof exists.
- No generic AI hype.
- No PwC commercial linkage.
- CTA is appropriate.
- Style sounds like Andrew.

## Output

Return:

- publish / fix / backlog;
- required edits in priority order;
- no more than 3 fixes unless the asset is fundamentally wrong.
